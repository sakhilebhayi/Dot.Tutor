This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Tutor, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Tutor and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Tutor processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information:** your name and email address from your Dot Ecosystem (InfoDot) account, and the record of your signed-in sessions.

**Learning profile:** your country, curriculum, level and subjects, the languages you understand and want to be taught in, and your month and year of birth (never the day). We use your age group to apply the protections for learners under 18.

**Guardians:** for a learner under 18, the link to their parent or guardian, the consents the guardian gave and the approvals they decided.

**Tutor information,** if you teach: your profile, photo, qualifications and the evidence you upload for review, teaching subjects, levels and languages, availability, offerings and prices, and the bank account your earnings are paid to (stored encrypted; only the last four digits are shown).

**Lessons and learning:** bookings and their notes, lesson files, ratings and reviews, course enrolments and progress, quiz attempts, flashcards, posts in subject rooms, and messages. Contact details are removed from notes and posts by learners under 18 before they are stored or shown.

**Payments:** orders, receipts, refunds and earnings. Card details are entered on Paystack's page and are never stored by Dot.Tutor.

**Study assistant:** when the assistant is switched on and you use it, your questions and its answers.

**Technical information:** IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- match learners with tutors and show why each one fits;
- run bookings, lessons, courses, rooms and messages, and the payments for them;
- review tutors' qualifications and teaching languages before labelling them verified;
- ask a guardian to approve what a learner under 18 books, pays for or joins;
- handle reports and keep learners, especially children, safe; and
- keep Dot.Tutor secure and prevent abuse.

## 4. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Tutor (or the other way round), a short-lived, single-use token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 5. Who can see your information

An approved tutor's storefront (name, photo, headline, introduction, subjects, levels, teaching languages, verification labels, offerings and prices, published reviews) can be read by anyone, including people who are not signed in. Contact details written in a tutor's own text are hidden from visitors and from learners under 18. A lesson, its notes and its files are visible only to the people in that lesson. Other learners see you by your first name and initial. A guardian sees the lessons, payments and approvals of the learner they are linked to. Our operators can see what they need for verification, payments, support and safety, and their access to a person's account is recorded.

Some services we use process information for us: InfoDot for sign-in, Paystack for payments, and, only when the study assistant is switched on, the AI provider that answers your question.

## 6. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and profile while your account is open. You can close your account from "Your data and account". Where nothing depends on your account it is deleted; otherwise it is closed: you can no longer sign in, your profile, languages and settings are erased and your name is replaced. Payment records are kept as tax law requires, and safety records are kept with the people they protect.

## 7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including private file storage, access checks on every lesson, message and file, encryption of bank details, and single-use tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you (you can download a copy from "Your data and account");
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

Dot.Tutor uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.
