# R-47 decisions log

Format: `D-n (agent, date): decision. Why.` Each agent appends in its own number block (docs/R47_ARCHITECTURE.md section 1).

D-1 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Build in waves of parallel agents on worktrees off the trunk `feat/r47-tutor`, as Dot.Ehail R-46 did. Why: the brief spans a dozen domains; one contract plus owned paths lets them proceed in parallel without collisions.
D-2 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Keep the shared-host constraints: no daemons, polling instead of websockets, cron-driven queue. Why: the live host is shared cPanel, the same as every other Dot app.
D-3 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): The academic structure is data (countries, curricula, frameworks, stages, levels, subject offerings), seeded with CAPS, IEB, a tertiary track and Cambridge. Why: brief section 5 forbids hardcoding one country's system.
D-4 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Paystack for payments, integer minor units, a double-entry ledger. Why: owner decision 2026-10-18 and the money rules learned on Dot.Ehail and Dot.Finance.
D-5 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Live lessons are native WebRTC with polling signalling (no TURN yet, honest fallback to the tutor's own meeting link). Why: Dot.Engage proved the approach on the same host; brief section 12 forbids fake video.
D-6 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): AI through an `AiTutor` interface with an Anthropic driver, off until the owner sets a key; Dot.Agents, Dot.Brain and Dot.Memory are not called because no documented cross-app API exists for this use. Why: brief sections 14 and 21 require genuine integrations only.
D-7 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Minors are protected server-side through one `SafeguardingGate` seam that every talk, meet, pay and publish action asks. Why: brief section 15; one gate is testable and cannot be skipped by a UI.

D-300 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Extend the existing tutor_profiles, tutor_sessions and session_ratings tables additively instead of new booking or review tables. Why: keeps every existing row, route and test (scheduling core, ratings) working and gives one history per lesson.
D-301 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Marketplace domain code in app/Domain/Marketplace; legacy controllers (confirm, cancel, complete) now delegate to BookingFlow. Why: one state machine, so a v2 paid booking cannot be confirmed past payment through the old button.
D-302 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): The marketplace depends on its own ports (AcademicScopePort, LanguageProfilePort, LearnerAgePort, GuardianApprovalsPort, SafeguardingPort, CheckoutPort, ReportSinkPort, AuditPort) with null defaults bound by bindIf, instead of creating files in the Academic, Safety or Money namespaces. The integration provider binds adapters onto the owners' interfaces. Why: two parallel agents creating the same interface file would conflict at merge, and an adapter absorbs signature differences (SafeguardingPort already mirrors safety's four-argument allows()).
D-303 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Verification levels peer_student_tutor, independent_tutor, verified_qualified_tutor, professional_educator, institution_affiliated, each tied to supporting qualification kinds; a level can only be assigned when a verified qualification of such a kind exists, and revoking the last one removes the level. Why: brief section 16, "based on evidence"; makes over-claiming impossible rather than discouraged.
D-304 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Evidence and photos on the private local disk under marketplace/, served by authorising controllers with nosniff; images are decoded and re-encoded to JPEG, PDFs checked by magic bytes; no virus scanning (not available on the host), stated on the operator page. Why: architecture section 2 files rule.
D-305 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): The legacy free-text qualifications column is shown as "Self-declared, not verified" and never feeds a level. Why: brief section 7, "clearly distinguish verified qualifications from unverified claims".
D-306 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Academic ids (curriculum_id, level ids) are stored as plain indexed integers without foreign keys until the academic tables are merged. Why: the academic tables do not exist on this branch; the integration step adds the keys.
D-307 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Offerings are priced in price_minor + currency, 15 to 240 minutes in 15-minute steps, archived rather than deleted; each booking snapshots its price. hourly_rate_minor and rate_minor companions are backfilled and kept in step with the decimals by model hooks. Why: money rule (integer minor units), history preserved.
D-308 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Matching is a weighted sum of nine named terms (scope 25, curriculum 10, language 15, availability 15, budget 10, help type 5, verification 8, reviews 7, new tutor 5) plus hard filters, with a hash tie-break; no boost column exists. Why: brief section 6 (explainable, not popularity or payment, fair to newcomers); documented in R47_MATCHING.md.
D-309 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Discovery listing order is a daily-seeded hash rotation, not rating or session count. Why: no tutor permanently first or last; rating ordering was the audit's "popularity" finding.
D-310 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): The brief's "requested" state is stored as the existing 'pending' value (SessionStatus::Requested is an alias) and reported as "requested" by the API; new states awaiting_guardian, awaiting_payment, in_progress, disputed are added; holdingTime now includes the awaiting and in-progress states. Why: no historic row rewritten; every existing caller keeps working.
D-311 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Group lessons are scheduled by the tutor (holding their time) and learners take seats; capacity is enforced by a conditional UPDATE on seats_taken < capacity, not only by a lock. Why: race-safe even on a stale read and on SQLite, which has no row locks.
D-312 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Idempotency-Key handled by one marketplace_idempotency_keys table with a unique (user, scope, key) index written in the resource's transaction; same key + different body is 422. Why: architecture section 2; one mechanism for bookings, seats, series, packages and group scheduling.
D-313 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Paid one-to-one bookings move to awaiting_payment on the tutor's acceptance with a hold of 48 hours (never past the start); group seats hold 30 minutes; expired holds are released every five minutes by tutor:expire-marketplace-holds. Free and package-covered bookings are confirmed on acceptance. Why: a request should not tie up money before the tutor agrees, and a seat must not be held forever.
D-314 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Cancellation policy (notice hours, late refund %) lives on the offering; the refund due is computed and recorded on the booking event, and the money domain executes refunds. Why: marketplace owns the terms, money owns movement.
D-315 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Weekly series book the same local wall-clock time each week, skip clashing weeks and report them; at least one week must book. Why: a blocked holiday should not cancel a term of lessons.
D-316 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Reviews: one per booking (unique key widened to session + reviewer so each group seat can review), held on arrival when they contain an email, link or phone number, held after three distinct reports, only published reviews count; tutor may reply once (editable) without contact details. Why: brief section 15 (fake reviews, off-platform contact) and "show counts honestly".
D-317 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): JSON API under /api/tutor inside the web group (session auth or Sanctum, CSRF on), errors rendered as {error, message} by the provider for that path only. Why: one-line routes rule; first-party screens use the session. Token-only POST clients are not supported yet.
D-318 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): Payment start with the null Checkout answers 503 payments_not_configured and leaves the booking awaiting_payment. Why: honesty rule: no simulated success.
D-319 (marketplace, 2026-11-10): No new dependency. Image handling uses PHP GD (present on the host build used here).
