# R-47 decisions log

Format: `D-n (agent, date): decision. Why.` Each agent appends in its own number block (docs/R47_ARCHITECTURE.md section 1).

D-1 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Build in waves of parallel agents on worktrees off the trunk `feat/r47-tutor`, as Dot.Ehail R-46 did. Why: the brief spans a dozen domains; one contract plus owned paths lets them proceed in parallel without collisions.
D-2 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Keep the shared-host constraints: no daemons, polling instead of websockets, cron-driven queue. Why: the live host is shared cPanel, the same as every other Dot app.
D-3 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): The academic structure is data (countries, curricula, frameworks, stages, levels, subject offerings), seeded with CAPS, IEB, a tertiary track and Cambridge. Why: brief section 5 forbids hardcoding one country's system.
D-4 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Paystack for payments, integer minor units, a double-entry ledger. Why: owner decision 2026-10-18 and the money rules learned on Dot.Ehail and Dot.Finance.
D-5 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Live lessons are native WebRTC with polling signalling (no TURN yet, honest fallback to the tutor's own meeting link). Why: Dot.Engage proved the approach on the same host; brief section 12 forbids fake video.
D-6 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): AI through an `AiTutor` interface with an Anthropic driver, off until the owner sets a key; Dot.Agents, Dot.Brain and Dot.Memory are not called because no documented cross-app API exists for this use. Why: brief sections 14 and 21 require genuine integrations only.
D-7 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Minors are protected server-side through one `SafeguardingGate` seam that every talk, meet, pay and publish action asks. Why: brief section 15; one gate is testable and cannot be skipped by a UI.

D-400 (safety, 2026-11-10): The safety domain lives in `app/Domain/Safety` with its own provider (`TutorSafetyServiceProvider`), views loaded from `app/Domain/Safety/resources/views` (namespace `safety::`), a new `config/safety.php`, one migration `2026_11_10_400000_create_safety_tables.php`, and the private `safety` disk registered in the provider. Why: keeps every file inside the owned paths; shared files got one line each.
D-401 (safety, 2026-11-10): Age bands child under 13, teen 13 to 17, adult 18+, thresholds in config. Why: architecture section 2; the child/teen split decides how much a guardian approves, the law treats both as children.
D-402 (safety, 2026-11-10): Unknown age is treated as adult by default (`SAFETY_UNKNOWN_AGE_POLICY=adult`), `restrict` treats it as a child; anyone with an active guardian link is a minor. Why: no learner has a date of birth yet (academic onboarding is being built), and treating every existing user as a child would stop the live app; the switch is an owner item once dates of birth are collected.
D-403 (safety, 2026-11-10): Date-of-birth trust order: operator-verified, then guardian-confirmed (both in `safety_age_records`), then self-declared via the `BirthDateSource` seam (null default; academic binds it at integration). Why: a guardian's or an operator's word should outrank a child's own entry.
D-404 (safety, 2026-11-10): Guardian invitation by temporary signed URL plus a hashed 48-character token, 72 hours, five a day; the signed-in email must equal the invited address; the guardian confirms the learner's date of birth; consent stores version and a sha256 of the exact text; only the guardian or an operator revokes. Why: POPIA s35(1)(a) competent-person consent needs to be attributable and provable, and a minor must not be able to remove oversight.
D-405 (safety, 2026-11-10): Approvals: a child's booking, purchase, paid room and live lesson always wait; a teen's default to booking, purchase and paid room (not live lesson) and a guardian may change them, strictest guardian wins; requests expire after 72 hours (read as expired at once, swept hourly). Why: brief section 15 (guardian-approved bookings and payments) with room for teen autonomy.
D-406 (safety, 2026-11-10): `SafeguardingGate::allows()` takes a fourth `array $context` argument and an eighth ability `upload`; a `Decision` carries reasons and requirements; each ability is also a Laravel ability `safety.<ability>`, and `safety.operate` marks safeguarding staff (is_platform_operator until ops roles land). Contract change for every owner. Why: live lessons need `record`, publishing needs `commercial` and `audience`, and policies elsewhere can use `Gate::authorize`.
D-407 (safety, 2026-11-10): Conversations always have a context (booking, room, support, direct); direct conversations are refused whenever a minor is involved with an adult, and whenever a child is involved at all. Why: brief section 15 restricted communication; peer chat between children belongs in moderated rooms.
D-408 (safety, 2026-11-10): Every adult in an exchange with a minor (booking, messaging, live lesson, joining a room with minor bands) must hold a current screening, except operators and that minor's guardians. Why: brief section 15 appropriate tutor screening; one rule instead of per-feature exceptions.
D-409 (safety, 2026-11-10): Contact details are redacted by pattern in conversations with a minor (and in profile text via `ContactPrivacy`), with a note saying why; the original is kept encrypted for safeguarding staff only. Meeting links (Zoom, Meet, Teams, Skype) are hidden too. Why: moving a child off-platform is a grooming pattern; the evidence must survive for a report.
D-410 (safety, 2026-11-10): A model observer on the existing `TutorSession` refuses bookings the gate denies, requests guardian approval for a minor's booking, and refuses confirmation before approval. It uses queries, not relations, so it never changes what booking code sees. Cross-domain: marketplace booking v2 should call the gate itself; the observer remains a backstop. Why: brief section 25 requires server-side enforcement a UI cannot bypass, on the booking path that exists today.
D-411 (safety, 2026-11-10): Screening is operator-recorded with evidence, reviewed by a second operator (four eyes, `SAFETY_SCREENING_FOUR_EYES`), cannot be cleared without evidence, and the required kinds default to the Child Protection Register Part B and the National Register for Sex Offenders. Why: neither register has a public lookup, so nothing can be automated honestly; the brief forbids claiming it.
D-412 (safety, 2026-11-10): Report priority: inappropriate contact with a minor is critical (1 h) and opens a critical incident at once; harassment or other involving a minor is high (4 h); spam low (72 h); else normal (24 h). A cheating request opens an academic integrity case. Why: child-safety fast path in brief section 15 and the integrity policy.
D-413 (safety, 2026-11-10): Incidents escalate one level (cap 3) each time an unacknowledged incident's timer runs out, checked every five minutes by cron, and every operator gets a database notification. Email and push alerts wait for the comms domain. Why: shared host, cron only; no fake paging.
D-414 (safety, 2026-11-10): The audit log is append-only and hash-chained, with a daily verify command; no foreign key on the actor so the log outlives accounts; PostgreSQL writers take an advisory lock. Why: brief section 15 audit trails; the chain shows tampering around the model.
D-415 (safety, 2026-11-10): JSON errors for `/safety/*` are rendered `{error, message}` by `renderable` hooks registered in the safety provider, because `bootstrap/app.php` renders JSON only for `api/*`. Why: architecture section 2 error convention without editing a shared file.
D-416 (safety, 2026-11-10): Safeguarding evidence rows (reports, incidents, screening, moderation actions, restrictions, integrity cases) restrict deletion of the people they name; message senders and conversation creators null out instead. Why: deleting an account must not erase what happened to a child; POPIA allows keeping records needed for legal obligations, which a lawyer should confirm.
D-417 (safety, 2026-11-10): Room conversations and room joins go through the `RoomDirectory` and `AgeGatedSpace` seams; the null directory knows no rooms. Why: rooms are wave 2 (community); no fake rooms.
D-418 (safety, 2026-11-10): Operators read a conversation they are not in only when it is a support thread or something in it was reported, and each read is audited. Why: privacy of learners' messages; safeguarding access only where there is a reason.
D-419 (safety, 2026-11-10): The guardian dashboard's purchases come from purchase and paid-room approvals, with `orders_connected: false` until the money domain is wired. Why: honesty rule; an empty order list would read as "nothing bought".
D-420 (safety, 2026-11-10): Message attachments: five per message, 5 MB, sniffed type allowlist (PDF, PNG, JPEG, text), images re-encoded with GD to strip metadata, private disk, streamed with nosniff and no-store. No virus scanner on the host. Why: architecture section 2 file rules; location metadata in a child's photo is a risk.
D-500 (money, 2026-11-10): The money domain lives in `app/Domain/Money` with its own `TutorMoneyServiceProvider` (in the domain folder) and views under `app/Domain/Money/Views` (`money::`). Why: owned paths only; no edits to shared view folders.
D-501 (money, 2026-11-10): Paystack hosted checkout (initialize, then server-side verify); no saved cards. Paid only when verify says success with matching reference, amount and currency. Why: brief 17 (server-side verification); no card tokens to protect.
D-502 (money, 2026-11-10): The platform collects on behalf of tutors: tutor earnings are a liability (`tutor_pending`, `tutor_payable`), commission is revenue, Paystack's fee is a platform expense unless `MONEY_PASS_GATEWAY_FEE_PERCENT` is set. Why: one integration that works for unregistered peer tutors; matches Dot.Ehail.
D-503 (money, 2026-11-10): Commission is taken from the tutor's selling price after their own discount; platform-funded coupons and credits do not reduce what the tutor earns. Why: a tutor should never pay for the platform's promotion.
D-504 (money, 2026-11-10): Default commission 15% (config placeholder, not a market figure) plus versioned, never-edited commission rules per category in the database; each order line stores the rule it was priced with. Why: brief asks for clear commission; history must not change.
D-505 (money, 2026-11-10): Lesson earnings become available 24 hours after the lesson is completed; digital items after a 7-day refund window; no-shows and cancelled paid lessons go to finance review. Why: complaint and refund windows before money leaves.
D-506 (money, 2026-11-10): Payouts are a manual weekly CSV batch (minimum R100) that finance marks paid; Paystack Transfers built but off. Why: transfers need a Registered Business with a funded balance (owner item).
D-507 (money, 2026-11-10): Refunds, credits, platform coupons, commission rules and payouts sit behind gate `money.finance` (platform operator, narrowed by `MONEY_FINANCE_OPERATOR_EMAILS`) until the ops agent adds the finance role; the refund service re-checks the gate. Why: brief 17 forbids unauthorised refunds.
D-508 (money, 2026-11-10): A refund reverses its share of the sale (tutor share from pending or available, platform share to a `refunds` contra account, platform discount and credit back); Paystack's original fee stays a platform cost. Why: ledger reversal with an auditable trail; Paystack does not return fees.
D-509 (money, 2026-11-10): Guardian approval through money-owned seams `PurchaseApprovals` and `BuyerAge` (defaults: adults pass, child and teen held, unknown age passes unless `MONEY_HOLD_UNKNOWN_AGE=true`); held orders are kept 7 days; `Orders::recordGuardianDecision` takes the answer. Why: the safety paths are not mine; money holds no date of birth today. Integration must bind the safety adapters (risk named in R47_MONEY section 6).
D-510 (money, 2026-11-10): Money actions are audited through a `MoneyAudit` seam whose default writes one structured log line (ids only). Why: Safety\Audit is not built yet.
D-511 (money, 2026-11-10): Recurring subscriptions are not built in wave 1. Why: saved-card mandates, dunning and minors' recurring charges need a guardian mandate that does not exist; packages and bundles cover the need honestly.
D-512 (money, 2026-11-10): Disputes are recorded from webhooks; finance sets the outcome and a lost dispute posts the loss once. Why: Paystack's dispute resolution wording is unverified here.
D-513 (money, 2026-11-10): Promotional credits do not expire and are reserved by open orders. Why: simpler, consumer-friendly, no double spend.
D-514 (money, 2026-11-10): Money is shown as `R 1 250.00` with narrow no-break spaces (symbol and thousands) and a decimal point. Why: common South African online usage; never wraps on small screens.
D-515 (money, 2026-11-10): Receipts use gapless `TUT-` numbers and a frozen snapshot; operator identity from config (missing values left off); a VAT line only when registered and a number is set, prices VAT-inclusive. Why: honesty about tax status.
D-516 (money, 2026-11-10): Wave-1 pages reuse the incumbent `t-*` classes of the signed-in layout; wave 2 rebuilds them on the ds design system. Why: functional minimum without inventing a parallel style.
D-517 (money, 2026-11-10): Lessons are purchasable through an adapter over `tutor_sessions` (`BookingPurchasable`, registered with `registerIfMissing` so marketplace can register its own `booking` first). Why: real end-to-end payment for bookings now, without editing marketplace files.
D-518 (money, 2026-11-10): `Idempotency-Key` is required (428) on order creation, refunds and credits; webhooks are identified by a hash of event, object and status. Why: architecture section 2; Paystack sends no event id.
D-519 (money, 2026-11-10): The Paystack webhook is `POST /api/tutor/money/webhooks/paystack`, CSRF- and session-exempt, signature on the raw body, 1 MB cap. Why: owner sets it in the Paystack dashboard.
D-520 (money, 2026-11-10): No new dependencies. Why: Laravel's HTTP client, encryption and queue cover the domain.
