# R-47 decisions log

Format: `D-n (agent, date): decision. Why.` Each agent appends in its own number block (docs/R47_ARCHITECTURE.md section 1).

D-1 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Build in waves of parallel agents on worktrees off the trunk `feat/r47-tutor`, as Dot.Ehail R-46 did. Why: the brief spans a dozen domains; one contract plus owned paths lets them proceed in parallel without collisions.
D-2 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Keep the shared-host constraints: no daemons, polling instead of websockets, cron-driven queue. Why: the live host is shared cPanel, the same as every other Dot app.
D-3 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): The academic structure is data (countries, curricula, frameworks, stages, levels, subject offerings), seeded with CAPS, IEB, a tertiary track and Cambridge. Why: brief section 5 forbids hardcoding one country's system.
D-4 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Paystack for payments, integer minor units, a double-entry ledger. Why: owner decision 2026-10-18 and the money rules learned on Dot.Ehail and Dot.Finance.
D-5 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Live lessons are native WebRTC with polling signalling (no TURN yet, honest fallback to the tutor's own meeting link). Why: Dot.Engage proved the approach on the same host; brief section 12 forbids fake video.
D-6 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): AI through an `AiTutor` interface with an Anthropic driver, off until the owner sets a key; Dot.Agents, Dot.Brain and Dot.Memory are not called because no documented cross-app API exists for this use. Why: brief sections 14 and 21 require genuine integrations only.
D-7 (coordinator, 2026-11-10): Minors are protected server-side through one `SafeguardingGate` seam that every talk, meet, pay and publish action asks. Why: brief section 15; one gate is testable and cannot be skipped by a UI.
D-600 (learning, 2026-11-10): All learning tables are prefixed `learning_`; academic ids (curriculum, level, topic) are plain indexed columns without foreign keys, subjects reference the existing `subjects` table. Why: the academic tables do not exist yet and belong to another owner; the integration step can add constraints.
D-601 (learning, 2026-11-10): The learning domain depends on its own narrow ports in `Learning\Contracts\Seams` (AcademicScopeLookup, EntitlementLookup, CheckoutGateway, ContentReportSink, SafeguardingCheck, AuditTrail, TutorStanding), bound with `bindIf` to honest defaults, instead of defining the owners' interfaces in their paths. Why: parallel agents defining the same interface files would collide at merge; the wave-2 integration provider binds small adapters to the real Academic/Money/Safety/Marketplace seams.
D-602 (learning, 2026-11-10): Course content is versioned by immutable published snapshots; enrolments pin a version and learners opt in to the newest one; files of a course that has ever been published are never deleted. Why: brief 8 asks that edits do not break enrolled learners; snapshots make that exact and testable.
D-603 (learning, 2026-11-10): Paid access is re-checked against the entitlement on every request, not cached on the enrolment. Why: a refund in the money domain must end access immediately without a sync job.
D-604 (learning, 2026-11-10): Media is served by user-bound, 10-minute signed URLs that are still re-authorised per request, with range support from Symfony's BinaryFileResponse. Why: `<video>` and `<img>` need URLs, while access must stay server-side; signing alone would let a leaked link work for its lifetime.
D-605 (learning, 2026-11-10): Uploads are typed by content (finfo plus structural checks), images re-encoded with GD, macro-enabled and legacy binary Office files refused. Why: architecture "Files" rule; macros are the main document-borne threat and there is no virus scanner on the host.
D-606 (learning, 2026-11-10): Publishing a course requires an approved tutor profile (read from `tutor_profiles`) and the safeguarding `publish` check; drafts are open to any signed-in user. Why: brief 16 quality rules; drafting costs nothing and helps tutors start while verification runs.
D-607 (learning, 2026-11-10): Certificates exist only when the course sets a completion rule; the public check shows initials unless the holder opts in to the full name, and says it is not an accredited qualification. Why: brief 8 "where supported"; POPIA treats children's data as special, so the public page minimises by default.
D-608 (learning, 2026-11-10): Course reviews need an active enrolment and at least one completed lesson, one per learner (updatable). Why: brief 8 "review courses" with verified participation; one completed lesson stops drive-by ratings.
D-609 (learning, 2026-11-10): Quality tiers "reviewed" and "officially sourced" are set only by a recorded review with the reviewer's name; reviewers are platform operators (not the owner) until the ops agent adds a reviewer role. Why: brief 9 "never display a verification label unless a real review process has occurred".
D-610 (learning, 2026-11-10): Past exam papers are accepted only as "official source" with an https publisher link, free, and held for review before anyone else sees them; third-party "all rights reserved" material is refused; only own work can be sold, never public-domain or official material. Why: brief 9 copyright rule.
D-611 (learning, 2026-11-10): A complete copyright notice (rights holder, work link, good-faith statement) hides the resource immediately; the owner may counter; an operator upholds or dismisses; an upheld file's SHA-256 cannot be uploaded again. Why: ECT Act s77 take-down practice (desk reading, not legal advice); abuse is limited by the required fields, a rate limit and operator restoration.
D-612 (learning, 2026-11-10): Marking is server-side; multi-select is all-or-nothing; answer keys never leave the server before submission; time limits use the server clock with a 30-second grace and late answers cannot pass; questions are retired, not edited. Why: brief 13 and the cheating problem; partial credit on multi-select rewards ticking everything.
D-613 (learning, 2026-11-10): Topic mastery is recomputed from attempt answers on every read (window 10, minimum evidence 3, trend over halves of the last 20 with a 15-point threshold, all in `config/learning.php`); insights are fixed templates labelled "computed from your answers, not AI". The creator's own attempts are not evidence. Why: brief 13 asks for insights grounded in real answers and a clear line between guidance and assessed results.
D-614 (learning, 2026-11-10): Flashcards use SM-2. Why: published, simple, deterministic and testable.
D-615 (learning, 2026-11-10): Tutors may assign assessments only to learners they teach (a confirmed or completed session, or an active enrolment in their course), plus the safeguarding `assign_work` check. Why: no tutor should push work to arbitrary accounts, especially children.
D-616 (learning, 2026-11-10): External videos are links, not iframes, and are labelled unprotected; uploaded video has no transcoding. Why: CSP is not yet enforced and the shared host has no ffmpeg or workers.
D-617 (learning, 2026-11-10): The JSON API shares controllers and route definitions with the web screens (session auth with CSRF); bearer-token clients are not offered. Why: one authorisation path to test; turning off CSRF for cookie sessions would be unsafe.
D-618 (learning, 2026-11-10): `DemoLearningSeeder` (demo tutor, learner, published course, practice quiz; refuses production) for local and E2E checks. Why: local registration is closed; brief honesty rule keeps demo data out of production.
