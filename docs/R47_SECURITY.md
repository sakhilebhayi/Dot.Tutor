# R-47 Dot.Tutor: security, child safety, payment integrity and privacy review

Owner: security agent (branch `feat/r47-security`, decisions D-1500 to D-1526). Method: the `security-audit` skill in guidance mode (D-1500), with its evidence bar: every finding below names a lower-trust principal, the boundary crossed and the concrete result, was traced in code (three read-only hunts by sub-agents, each candidate re-read and confirmed here before fixing) and, at medium and above, is fixed with a regression test in `tests/Feature/Security/` (or the domain test named). Severity follows the skill's anchors: **high** = an explicit control fully defeated with real consequences (account takeover, a child's protections lifted, money redirected); **medium** = a real boundary violation with limited reach or uncommon preconditions; **low** = minor disclosure or effort for little gain. Brief sections covered: 8, 9, 15, 17, 22, 26. The privacy record is `docs/R47_PRIVACY.md`. No legal compliance claim is made anywhere in this document.

## 2. Accepted or left to the owner (low, recorded)

- **Guardian identity.** A determined minor can still create a second InfoDot account with an address they control and invite it as a parent. The emailed token is not proof the parent read it either: the invitation response also returns the link to the learner (`accept_url`, kept for slow mail). Mitigated by S-01/S-06 (proven address, first guardian's birth date stands, existing guardians told). Owner item: guardian identity verification (for example an operator check for a first guardian, or a payment card in the guardian's name).
- **First declaration.** A child can declare an adult birth year the first time they are asked. Inherent to self-declaration; reporting and moderation remain. Owner item.
- **Hold vs dispute window.** The earnings hold is 24 hours after delivery while disputes may be opened for 7 days (`marketplace.dispute_window_days`); a dispute after release becomes a reversal against future earnings (S-10 covers disputes inside the hold). Owner decision: lengthen `MONEY` hold or accept.
- **Partial refunds rounding** (`PostingRules`): repeated partial refunds of a line paid partly with credit can return up to half a cent per refund more credit than spent. Finance-only action. Money owner.
- **Coupon reserved by a held order** for 7 days (`Orders`, guardian approval): limited coupons can be exhausted by minors' held orders. Money owner.
- **Attempts and certificates after a refund.** An attempt already open can still be submitted, past results read, and certificates are never revoked (`revoked_at` unused). Learning owner.
- **Study groups.** Children's group names, topics, schedule and "where" notes are redacted for contact details but not pre-moderated; physical meet-up notes among minors are possible. Community and safeguarding owner.
- **Classroom signalling** (`signal`, `board`) skips re-admission until the next poll; established peer-to-peer media are not cut by the server in any case.
- **SSO same-site fallback.** With neither `Origin` nor `Referer`, `/auth/ecosystem` accepts `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-site` from any sibling subdomain (login CSRF into the attacker's own account). Ecosystem-wide decision.
- **Classroom page side effect.** Calling a classroom endpoint for any lesson id creates its classroom row (no data exposed). 
- **No throttle** on library reviews, recommendations and withdrawals (moderated content, low value).
- **Restriction evasion.** A suspended person can close their account and return with a new InfoDot address; operators can match the closing address kept in the audit log.
- **`/up`** is the framework's health page; its CDN styling is blocked by the enforced CSP (monitors read the status code).
- **Production `.env`.** The live host's `.env` may still carry `SECURITY_CSP_REPORT_ONLY=true` from the earlier example; owner: set `false` (or remove it) at deploy, and confirm `SESSION_SECURE_COOKIE` (now defaulted on an https `APP_URL`). The preflight warns on the cookie.
- **Unknown age in production.** With the `ask` default, existing live users without a birth date (and not tutors, operators or guardians) are asked for a month and year of birth before they book, pay, message, join rooms, meet or publish. Intended; the owner may set `SAFETY_UNKNOWN_AGE_POLICY=adult` only as a temporary, recorded exception.
- **No virus scanner** on the shared host (architecture section 2); uploads are sniffed, size-capped and images re-encoded.

## 3. Authorisation, mechanically (scope 1)

`tests/Feature/Security/RouteAuthorizationMapTest.php` (four tests, about 4 s):

1. Every one of the ~690 routes is classified: `PUBLIC` (21 entries, each with its reason), guest-only, or behind sign-in; a new route that is none of these fails; stale entries fail.
2. A guest gets nothing (no 2xx, no 5xx) from every non-public route, with real ids from the seeded world.
3. **The stranger sweep.** The Demo seeders (student, tutor workspace, learning, community, classroom, ops) build other people's world; one row of every model a route binds that nobody seeded is filled from the schema. The stranger is at once an adult learner, an approved tutor and the guardian of their own child. Every signed-in route that carries an id (over 400) is called with someone else's ids, reads first; rate limiting is off for the sweep, and a 422 is retried with a body built from the validation errors (three rounds) so validation cannot hide a missing ownership check. Nothing may succeed (no 2xx) and nothing may crash (no 5xx), except the `SHARED` list (published storefronts, courses, rooms, library items, preview lessons, own progress, joining an open room, reporting), each entry with its reason. Result after the fixes: 216 refused (403), 88 not found, 10 conflicts, 31 validation refusals reviewed by hand (all decide ownership in the service: `Posts::mayResolve`, `StudyGroups::mustBeMember`, `LibraryService::counterNotice`, `TranslationAssistant::review`, …), and only SHARED routes answered 2xx.
4. Operator routes (the ops permission matrix, `RoutePermissions`) refuse every non-operator (403/404). Role-by-role operator access is the ops agent's `OperatorRouteMatrixTest`.

Mutation check: `ConversationPolicy::view` forced to allow made the sweep fail on `GET /safety/conversations/1`; reverted.

Owner isolation covered by the sweep: tutor A against tutor B's bookings, students (`teach.students.show`), earnings, payouts, offerings, series, files, courses, banks and assessments; a learner against another's attempts, conversations, orders, receipts, package purchases, seats, flashcards and AI interactions; a guardian against unlinked children (`guardian.child`) and other guardians' approvals and links; paid course content, library downloads and every classroom endpoint against a non-entitled stranger.

## 4. Web security (scope 5)

- **CSP, enforced** (D-1514): `default-src 'self'; script-src 'self' 'nonce-…' [+ 'unsafe-eval' only on pages that run Livewire/Alpine]; style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'; font-src 'self' data:; img-src 'self' data: blob:; media-src 'self' blob:; connect-src 'self'; worker-src 'self'; manifest-src 'self'; frame-src 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'self'; form-action 'self' https://checkout.paystack.com <InfoDot>; report-uri /csp-report`. WebRTC peer connections and STUN are not governed by `connect-src`. Escape hatch `SECURITY_CSP_REPORT_ONLY=true`.
- **Render sweep** (`CspRenderSweepTest`): every GET page that renders HTML for a guest and eleven Demo personas (learners, a Grade 3 child, a teen, guardians, three tutors, an ops admin, a moderator), over 150 pages: every inline script carries the response's nonce, no inline event handler, no `javascript:` link, nothing from another host, Alpine/Livewire pages carry `'unsafe-eval'`.
- **Browser pass** (Claude Browser pane, local server on 8720 with the Demo seeders, enforced policy): as a learner (home, find, storefront, course, preview lesson, library, rooms, classroom list, messages, Livewire profile, purchases, account, notification settings, inbox, AI assistant, practice, progress, lessons, legacy tutor page), a tutor (all 15 workspace pages, studio), a classroom tutor (the live classroom pre-join page: camera and microphone requested, which the pane itself blocks), an ops admin (16 command-centre pages, Livewire profile) and a guardian (both guardian pages), and the landing page: **zero CSP violations** in the console and no `csp.violation` line in the server log; a deliberately injected inline script was blocked and reported, proving the capture works. Alpine and Livewire initialise on the profile page.
- **Permissions-Policy**: camera, microphone, display-capture only on `classroom.show` (`(self)`), denied everywhere else; geolocation, payment, usb denied. COOP `same-origin`, nosniff, `X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN`, strict-origin-when-cross-origin, HSTS 180 days on https.
- **CSRF**: every state-changing web route is in `web` with CSRF except the signed Paystack webhook, `/auth/ecosystem` (token redeemed server-to-server, origin checked) and `/csp-report` (no session).
- **Sessions**: http-only, SameSite lax, Secure on https by default, database driver, `AuthenticateSession` (a password change ends other sessions); InfoDot adoption deletes sessions.
- **Rate limits** present on login, two-factor, SSO, AI (`ai-requests`), uploads (`uploads`, `learning-uploads`, classroom files), messaging (`safety-messages` + per-user service limits), matching (`marketplace-matching`), bookings, checkout and payments, reports, guardian invitations, community writes and polls, classroom poll/signal/board, data export (5/hour), account closure.
- **SSRF**: the only user-influenced outbound URL is a Web Push endpoint, limited to https on known push-service hosts (`comms.push.allowed_hosts`); InfoDot, Paystack, Notify and Analytics URLs come from configuration. External video and meeting links are never fetched.
- **Open redirects**: none (`safeRedirect` on SSO; `redirect()->away` only to Paystack's checkout host).
- **Errors**: `APP_DEBUG` in production fails `tutor:preflight`; JSON errors carry `{error, message}` only.

## 5. Reviewed and sound

- Paystack webhook: HMAC-SHA512 of the raw body with `hash_equals`, 1 MB bound, stored once per event key, every state change re-verified with Paystack; amount, currency and reference compared to the frozen payment; unknown references raised, never settled; personal data stripped from stored payloads.
- Checkout return re-verifies server-side; another user's reference is 404. Prices come only from the Purchasable; discounts and credits capped; coupon and credit races under row locks; order idempotency keys scoped per buyer.
- Refunds: finance only (route gate + service gate), bounded by refundable cash under row locks, idempotent, processed only on Paystack's confirmation; course access re-checked on every request.
- Ledger: balanced journals, non-negative lines, idempotency keys; payouts paid only after transfer verify.
- Files: private disks only, sniffed types, size caps, image re-encoding (learning, safety, marketplace, classroom; now lesson resources), random paths, authorise-then-stream with `nosniff`, `attachment`, `no-store`; learning media signed URLs bound to the user and re-authorised; nothing under `public/` shadows a route (deploy D-1903 fixed the classroom sprite).
- SSO: token only in a POST body (the old GET link is ignored), redeemed server-to-server with this platform's secret, single use.
- Mass assignment: `User::$fillable` is name, email, password; no request writes operator, screening, age band or guardian fields.
- Operator reads of a person (`ops.account.viewed`, `safety.account.viewed`) and screening evidence views are audited.
