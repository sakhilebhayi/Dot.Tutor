# R-47 accessibility and mobile usability (measured)

Owner: perf/a11y agent (wave 3), branch `feat/r47-perfa11y`, decisions D-1700 to D-1709. Brief section 23 and section
26 items 19 and 20. Method and scripts adapted from Dot.Ehail R-46 (`R46_A11Y.md`). Skills: `impeccable` (context
loaded, audit/harden within the ds world, craft floor read before every UI edit; recorded in `R47_SKILLS.md`).

## 1. Method

Real Chrome (installed Google Chrome through `playwright-core`, headless) against the dev server over the demo
database (every `Demo*` seeder; `sh scripts/a11y/serve.sh`). People sign in through the real form. axe-core and
playwright-core are pinned devDependencies (`axe-core` 4.14.0, `playwright-core` 1.64.1, MIT and Apache-2.0); nothing
loads from a CDN (D-1700).

| Script | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `scripts/a11y/sweep.mjs` | axe-core, tags wcag2a/aa, wcag21a/aa, wcag22aa and best practice, plus **AAA contrast on the young-learner step**; every GET page of every role and age band (guest; Grade 3 child, teen, matric, university and adult learners; course learner; tutor, studio tutor and a tutor still onboarding; guardian; operator; classroom learner), the five rendered error pages, and the **live classroom** with tutor and learner joined (fake camera and microphone), each at 390, 768 and 1440 px in light and dark. Also horizontal overflow, h1 count, duplicate ids, and touch targets under 24 px (44 px on the young step) by bounding box, excluding links inside sentences (WCAG 2.5.8 inline exception). JSON-only endpoints that live under page paths (`/learning/certificates`, `/learning/banks`, ...) are not pages and are left out. `--ci` fails on any serious or critical rule. |
| `scripts/a11y/keyboard.mjs` | Real key events. Pages (390 and 1440): the skip link is the first stop and moves focus to the content, the Tab walk ends or wraps without a trap, every stop is named, has a visible focus indicator, is not hidden under the sticky bars (2.4.11) and is not zero-sized. **Journeys by keyboard only**: book a lesson (storefront, offering, open time by arrow keys and Space, request), take a quiz (enrol, start, answer, submit, result), post a question in a room, join a classroom from the pre-join screen (and every live control named; microphone, camera, screen share, devices, leave and chat reachable), a guardian approves a purchase, an operator releases a held post. |
| `scripts/a11y/motion.mjs` | With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no running animation, no transition over 0.01 ms, no smooth scrolling, on eleven pages including the live classroom; the same pages with motion allowed are measured to show the check bites. |
| `scripts/a11y/young-copy.mjs` | The young step's visible copy: Flesch-Kincaid grade per page and every sentence over 12 words or with a four-syllable word, as a guide for the human review. |

## 3. What was fixed

- **Contrast (D-1701).** The safety frame, the learning-studio styles and the money styles now use design-system
  tokens instead of dark-shell hex colours, so they meet AA in both themes; legal pages follow the theme (surface
  token, prose strong/em/table colours); Jetstream's session list uses token colours.
- **Headings (D-1702).** Sign-in, password, two-factor, confirm and verify pages carry one h1 through
  `x-authentication-card title`; terms, privacy and cookie pages have an h1.
- **Names (D-1703).** Room language filters: the visible language name starts the accessible name, the state is in
  `aria-current` and screen-reader text; empty table headers are named (Actions, You are).
- **Target size (D-1704).** `resources/css/a11y.css`: stand-alone text links, header and footer links and the wordmark
  are at least 24 px tall and wide (vertical padding on inline links does not move the line), disclosures 24 px; on the
  young step every link, pill, disclosure and button reaches 44 px. Sign-in "remember me" box 24 px; the room "Remove"
  button and studio checkbox rows 24 px.
- **Focus (D-1705).** Scroll padding for the sticky top bar and the learner's bottom bar on phones, so a control
  reached by Tab is never hidden (2.4.11); date, time and file inputs show the focus ring (Chrome does not match
  `:focus-visible` inside their fields, and the base rule removed it).
- **Young-learner copy (D-1708).** The young step's home notice, need prompt, empty states, practice and progress text
  rewritten in short sentences (home Flesch-Kincaid 2.8 to 2.4, practice 1.7 to 1.9 with fewer long sentences,
  progress 2.8 to 3.0 with no hard sentences); the standard step keeps its copy.

Already in place and verified: reduced motion (a global rule in `ds/base.css`), named classroom controls and the
classroom's own reduced-motion handling, the skip link on every shell, captions and labels on forms, the whiteboard and
media controls named (live classroom states 0 violations at 390, 768 and 1440 in both themes).

## 4. The young-learner step against a Grade 1-3 reading level

After the rewrite, the young step's own pages score Flesch-Kincaid 1.9 to 3.4 (home 2.4, matching 2.3, practice 1.9,
progress 3.0, account 2.3, rooms 3.1, assistant 3.4). Still above Grade 3 and recorded for the owners of those
screens: the courses page (4.8: "certificate", "accredited qualification"), a tutor's storefront (4.5: the tutor's own
words and the cancellation policy), and the room rules ("safeguarding screening"). Two shared pages render in the
standard step for a young learner (`/comms/inbox` 7.9, `/safety/me` 3.7): the comms and safety screens do not yet
adapt to the young step (D-1709, recorded for those owners). Subject names such as "isiZulu First Additional Language"
are the curriculum's own names and are kept.

## 5. Recorded, not fixed (moderate or minor)

- `landmark-unique` (moderate) on `/teach/students`, `/money/earnings`, `/money/ops`: two regions with the same name.
- `page-has-heading-one` (moderate) on Jetstream's `/user/profile` (InfoDot is the sign-in; the page is legacy).
- One inline link in a sentence on `/ops/audit` under 24 px (allowed by the inline exception).
- Repeated safeguarding reads per request (performance, `R47_PERF.md` section 2.2).

## 6. Regression tests

Browser checks are scripts (they need Chrome and a running server). PHP tests cover the markup they rely on through the
existing screen tests (the suite passes unchanged: 956 tests on SQLite, 0 failed; PostgreSQL green). `npm run build`
and `php artisan view:cache` pass.

## 7. Limits, honestly

- No real screen reader session (VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA). Names, roles, focus and live regions were checked through
  axe and the accessibility tree, not by listening.
- No physical phone; touch emulation in desktop Chrome. Safari and Firefox were not run.
- The classroom media was Chrome's fake camera and microphone; real devices and a real peer connection over the
  internet were not tested here (the classroom agent's notes apply).
- The young-copy check is a heuristic guide; a teacher's reading of the young screens is still worth doing.
- Error pages are judged as rendered files (a running dev server cannot produce 500 or 503 on demand).
