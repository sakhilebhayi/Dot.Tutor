# R-47 tutor workspace (tutor UI agent, decisions D-1200 to D-1218)

Brief sections 7 (tutor entrepreneurship), 8 (course creation for first-timers), 16 (verification shown accurately), 17 (price, commission, net, pending and available, payout status), 18 (storefront, promotional and referral links, bundles, packages, analytics) and the tutor and course creator journeys of 25. Code: `app/Domain/Teach/**` (provider `TutorTeachServiceProvider`), `routes/tutor/teach.php`, `resources/views/teach/**`, `resources/js/teach/**`, migration `2026_11_12_120000_create_teach_tables.php`, seeder `DemoTutorWorkspaceSeeder`, tests `tests/Feature/Teach/*` and `tests/js/teach.test.mjs`.

The workspace owns no business rules. Every action goes through the marketplace, money, learning, academic and safety code (D-1203), which checks state, ownership and safeguarding again on the server. All screens sit in `<x-layouts::workspace>` and are built only from the `x-td.*` kit plus Tailwind utilities on `td-*` tokens; tables use the dense step.

## Journeys

- **Tutor journey (brief 25):** onboarding steps create the profile, scope, languages, evidence, availability and the first offering with its commission preview; send for review; once approved, requests arrive on Today and Requests, are accepted (payment hold) or declined with a reason; lessons are started, completed or marked no-show; earnings show pending then available; bank details and payouts show the payout status. Covered by `TutorWorkspaceTest` (onboarding, every screen, booking actions, earnings with a real Paystack-verified payment through the faked gateway, CSV, bank details, isolation) together with the integration agent's `TutorJourneyTest`.
- **Course creator journey:** create a course, add sections and lessons with uploads, set language and level, price it, publish when the checks pass, watch enrolments and progress, see sales and net. Covered by `CourseStudioAndMaterialsTest`.
- **Peer to professional:** the verification ladder and qualification statuses on the profile step; a peer student tutor is shown as such (neutral badge), never as qualified.

## Access and privacy

- Every query is scoped through the signed-in tutor's own profile (`TutorContext::profile`), never an id from the URL. Another tutor's booking, offering, student, note, payout, bank or course is 404 or 403 (tested in `test_a_tutor_never_sees_or_touches_another_tutors_business`, the bank/assessment checks in `CourseStudioAndMaterialsTest`).
- Students are only people who did business with the tutor; minors' requests waiting for a guardian are a number only (D-1208, tested).
- Notes are private to the tutor and say not to record health, family or contact details.
- The income CSV holds only the tutor's own earnings (tested), `no-store`, formula-neutral cells.

## Not built, or deliberately left out

- **Reschedule** as one action (the marketplace has none, D-1205).
- **Referral rewards** (money has no credit rule; links are attribution only, D-1209). **Storefront view analytics** (no tracking exists, and none was added).
- **Recurring subscriptions** (money D-511): weekly lessons and packages cover the use.
- **Mixed bundles of lessons and digital items:** money's bundle API exists (digital items only); the studio builds course bundles through learning. A tutor-facing screen for money's mixed digital bundles was not built.
- **Editing a lesson in place** in the course builder: lessons are removed and added again (learning's update route exists for a later pass). **Section reordering.**
- **Screening upload by tutors:** operators record screening off-platform (safety design).
- The ops UI showing the re-send note: stored and audited here; the operations screens belong to the ops agent.
- Paid-course coupons for courses are money coupons and apply at checkout; there is no per-course coupon screen beyond the general discount codes.
